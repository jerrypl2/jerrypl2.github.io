Research & experiments

AI-native RAN

Exploring learning-based approaches to radio resource management, including scheduling and link adaptation. My focus is on clear problem definitions, meaningful evaluation, and the path from a promising model to a useful system.

From product questions to learning problems

My background in wireless communications has shaped how I approach AI: begin with a concrete decision, understand the constraints, and define what a useful improvement would mean at the system level.

Areas of interest

  • Learning-based scheduling and link adaptation in radio access networks.
  • Using network observations and logs to develop useful representations and predictions.
  • Evaluation that connects model behavior with practical radio resource management goals.

How I want to work

I am organizing this direction around small experiments with clear baselines. Each experiment should state its assumptions, test a specific decision, and make limitations visible. The emphasis is on understanding what is worth building and why.

The next question

When does a learned decision improve the whole system enough to justify its additional complexity?
