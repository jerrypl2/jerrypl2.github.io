Ongoing exploration

Aethervale Academy

An exploration of AI-supported personal learning. I am developing ideas and experiences around adaptive learning, thoughtful evaluation, and long-term interaction between a learner and an AI companion.

The question

What could a personal university look like when learning begins with a person’s own questions, goals, and experience? Aethervale Academy is my place to explore that question through a developing learning experience.

What I am exploring

  • Learning journeys that begin with a real question and lead to something a learner can explain, use, or build.
  • Evaluation that makes progress visible and helps identify what to work on next.
  • An AI companion that can revise its understanding of a learner over time, while the learner also develops an understanding of the AI.

Where it stands

A working demo exists and continues to evolve. It is an exploration of learning experiences and system design. Access to the current academy is invitation-based.

The next question

How can a learning system help people become more capable and more independent as their relationship with AI deepens?
