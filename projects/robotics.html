Learning in practice

Robotics Learning Lab

Learning robotics through ROS 2 and Gazebo experiments, starting with simulation and basic control. The next direction is robotic manipulation and imitation learning, with LeRobot SO-101 as a possible learning platform.

Understanding through small experiments

I am learning robotics by building up from simple, observable tasks. My current practice uses ROS 2 Jazzy and Gazebo to connect commands, simulated motion, and feedback.

Practice so far

  • Setting up ROS 2 communication and running basic publisher–subscriber examples.
  • Exploring Gazebo simulations and sending velocity commands to a simulated vehicle.
  • Writing a simple command sequence to move forward, turn, and stop.

The next direction

I want to progress toward robotic manipulation and imitation learning, potentially using LeRobot SO-101. This is a learning direction I plan to explore, with reproducible experiments as the foundation.

The next question

How can I carry what I learn in simulation into increasingly realistic perception and control tasks?
